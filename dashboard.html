Price by Category

Average price per food category (all years)

This donut chart breaks down the average price per kilogram by food category (cereals, meat, fish, vegetables, etc.). The size of each slice represents relative price levels — larger slices indicate more expensive food groups on average. Hover to see exact PHP/kg values. Price Trends by Category (Year-over-Year)

Annual average price per category over time

This multi-line chart shows how each food category's average price has changed year over year. Each colored line represents a category (cereals, meat, vegetables, etc.). Rising lines indicate price inflation for that food group; flat lines indicate stable pricing. Look for crossovers where one category overtakes another, and sharp increases that may correlate with supply shocks or economic events like the 2020 pandemic.

Regional Price Comparison

Average retail prices by region (2020-2023)

This horizontal bar chart compares average retail food prices across all 17 Philippine regions. Longer bars mean higher average prices. Use the category dropdown to filter by food type. This reveals geographic price disparities — for example, remote regions (like ARMM or Cordillera) often have higher prices due to transportation costs, while regions with strong agricultural production may have lower prices.

Price Map

Geographic distribution of average prices (2020-2023). Larger = higher price.

This interactive map plots circle markers at each WFP-monitored locality across the Philippines. Marker size and color encode average price level: green = lower prices, yellow = moderate, red = higher prices. Click any marker to see the locality name, province, region, average price, and number of records. Zoom in to explore specific areas.
