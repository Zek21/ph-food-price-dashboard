CPU was faster on this compact graph

CPU inference was faster at every measured batch. At batch 1, median latency was 1.0352 ms on DirectML versus 0.1740 ms on CPU. These measurements apply to this tested compact graph on this host. Native GPU placement does not imply a speedup; these results do not establish performance for other graphs or training workloads.
